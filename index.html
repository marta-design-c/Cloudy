The problem

01

Big events, single wears

Prom, graduation balls, weddings and parties demand a fresh fit — worn once, then it's dead stock in the closet.

02

Trends move faster than budgets

Gen Z chases a new trend every week on social media, but fast fashion can't be bought fast enough to keep up affordably.

03

No app-based rental in the Baltics

Resale exists, and a handful of physical salons rent locally — but nobody offers on-demand rental across categories, cities or an app.

"Gen Z trusts creators over ads — the most influencer-driven generation in fashion history. That's exactly why this model wins with them."

How Kloudy works

Three taps to your next look

STEP 01

Rent

Browse looks curated for balls, parties, dates and everyday fits. Pick your rental window in seconds.

+More on renting

Filter by size, occasion, material and clothing type. Every listing shows the real rental price per day, the lender's rating and available sizes — so you know exactly what's coming before you commit.

STEP 02

Wear & post

Wear it, post it, get the likes. Swap for something new whenever the moment calls for it.

+More on wearing it out

Free delivery and pickup across Riga. Wear it for your full rental window, snap your look, and tag @kloudy for a chance to be featured on the feed.

STEP 03

Return or own

Free returns — or fell in love? Convert what you paid toward a discounted buy-out.

+More on returns & buy-outs

Drop your return at any partner locker within 24 hours of your window ending — no dry cleaning required. Loved it? Apply what you already paid toward a discounted buy-out any time before you return it.
